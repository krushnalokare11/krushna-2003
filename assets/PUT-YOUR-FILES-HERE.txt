Put these files in this folder (assets/):

  profile.jpg            your photo, square, about 800x800 px, under 300 KB
  cv.pdf                 your CV
  favicon.svg            already here - change the letter "A" to your initial
  project-1.jpg          optional project images, 16:9
  project-2.jpg
  fem-report.pdf(report)         optional linked PDFs
  fem-project-reference-research-paper.pdf(reference paper) 
  fem-presentation.pdf(presentation)
  nonlocal-fem-feti1.m(code)  

  thesis.pdf

Keep the file names exactly as written above, or change the paths in index.html
to match your own names. Names are case-sensitive on GitHub Pages: Profile.JPG
will NOT load if the HTML says profile.jpg.
